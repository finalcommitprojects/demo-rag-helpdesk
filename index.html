College helpdesk chatbot

Ask about the rules of a fictional college. Answers come from its handbook, with the page they're from.

How it works

  1. Ingest: the handbook PDFs are split into passages, each tagged with its document and page.
  2. Retrieve: your question is matched against every passage with BM25 ranking, plus a few campus synonyms (exam, SEE, test).
  3. Answer: the best passage is shown with its source. With a Gemini API key, the model writes a short answer from only those passages and cites them. If nothing matches well, it says the handbook doesn't cover it instead of guessing.
